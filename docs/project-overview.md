# Project overview

## Problem statement

Facilities lose time and accountability when maintenance requests arrive through disconnected channels. Teams need a shared view of what is broken, how urgent it is, who owns it, when it is due, and whether the same asset keeps failing.

## Objective

Build one understandable operations platform that turns a report into a measurable workflow while giving students a concrete demonstration of HTML/CSS, JavaScript, React, Spring Boot, JDBC, Java, and SQL.

## Users

- **User:** reports issues, follows progress, and views relevant assets.
- **Technician:** accepts jobs, records diagnosis and repair notes, and manages workload.
- **Administrator:** manages operations, assignments, assets, locations, SLAs, and analytics.

## Differentiator

FixFlow is an operations platform rather than a generic complaint portal. Its value comes from explainable prioritization, technician matching, SLA visibility, asset health, and recurring-failure analysis.
